# Safety Limitations

AeroGuard is an AI-assisted aviation anomaly detection and decision-support research prototype. It is not a certified aviation safety system and must not be used as the sole basis for operational, maintenance, air-traffic-control, or emergency decisions without appropriate certification, validation, redundancy, human oversight, and regulatory approval.

The built-in demo telemetry is explicitly simulated. Anomaly score, model confidence, operational severity, and accident probability are separate concepts; this project does not present an accident probability. Thresholds are configurable research defaults, not certified aviation limits.
